# TEST-002 / EXP-002 — Boat 1 initial flotation observation

**Test date:** 2026-11-21 (morning, Africa/Cairo), reported by the operator.  
**Platform:** Boat 1, inherited ASV; mapping to project Robot B remains provisional.  
**Test class:** First qualitative flotation / buoyancy observation in actual water.  
**Source:** Operator's direct statement on 2026-11-21: “This morning we tested buoyancy and it floated fine in water.”  
**Evidence:** No photograph, video, draft measurement, payload inventory or telemetry from *this specific test* has been submitted. The five earlier uploaded photos are dry-bench configuration images, **not evidence of this water test**.

## Observed result

**PASS — basic flotation, qualitative and user-reported:** the inherited vessel was placed in water and remained afloat sufficiently for the operator to describe flotation as successful.

## Interpretation and limits

This result confirms the prototype has **positive buoyancy under the unknown test configuration**. It does **not** establish hydrostatic stability margins, freeboard, reserve displacement, maximum payload, structural soundness, electronic enclosure waterproofing, dynamic propulsion behavior, or its ability to penetrate water hyacinth. The tested mass, load distribution, water type, duration and trim/heel were not recorded.

No motor-power or forward-motion claims are made from this test; wireless bench-driven propulsion is documented separately in [Boat 1 commissioning](BOAT1_LEGACY_ASV_2026-11-20.md).
